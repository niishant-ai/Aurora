# Three-minute judge walkthrough

Start on the home page. The role in the header should be **Editor**. Theme can be switched live; it persists.

## 0:00 — The promise
Read the hero. Point at the six-desk pipeline and the citation-lock pair: the public line keeps `78 ng m-3` and links to the Bharati passage.

## 0:25 — Search
Open Search. Run `black carbon albedo` in **Semantic**, then **Keyword**. Keyword highlights the span. Semantic still ranks the Himadri and Bharati soot notes above the neutron-monitor log. Open the Bharati aerosol report.

## 1:05 — Select
In the document, tick the black-carbon spike and the irradiance passage. Choose **Social post**. The sticky bar only appears after a selection.

## 1:25 — Generate
On the generate desk, set reading level to **Public** and press **Generate**. Show the lock check: each sentence has a verbatim source excerpt, and no new numeral appears. Press **Submit for review**.

## 1:55 — Roles
Open Review. As editor, you can submit and return, not publish. Switch the header to **Admin**. Approve the card, then publish it. Open the story and click a citation back to the passage.

## 2:25 — Calendar and ingest
Open Schedule. Drag an unscheduled card onto a day, or use the date field. Dashboard counts and the activity list move with the queue. If time remains, Ingest → **Load demo packet** files a Bharati note and embeds its paragraphs for the next search.

## What not to claim
Stock aurora and ice frames are illustrative. The reports are realistic mock records for the hackathon, not official MoES publications.
