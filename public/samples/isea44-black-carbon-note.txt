ISEA-44 Bharati intake note, 2025

Clear-sky aerosol optical depth at Bharati on 16 February 2025 was 0.037 at 500 nm.

Black carbon stayed below 20 ng m-3 except for a two-hour rise to 44 ng m-3 during a northerly wind.
